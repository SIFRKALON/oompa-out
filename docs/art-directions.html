First, the question you asked

Ready to build? Yes, with three small things locked first.

The design is complete enough to build a playable slice. What's still open are questions you answer by playing, not by planning. The code behind the map, scenes, dialogue and meters works the same whichever style we pick, so building can start while we decide on the art.

DIRECTION 1

Riso Union

Two to four spot inks, printed slightly out of line and dotted with halftone, like a union zine run off on the break-room copier. Constructivist wedges in candy-shop fluorescents.

Lineage
1930s CIO and WPA strike posters, Rodchenko and the Stenberg brothers, riso zine culture, and Pentiment, which turned print culture into the whole interface.
Why it spreads
Every screenshot looks like a poster. Ending cards become prints people share. Fluorescent pink on newsprint stops a scrolling thumb, and riso is everywhere in illustration right now.
Theme fit
The look of organizing literature is the look of the game. The Union Wall, the demands and the trial broadsheet are all things you'd actually print.
HTML cost
Low to medium. Flat vector shapes, mix-blend-mode: multiply, a halftone pattern. Art can be drawn in vector or scanned from real prints.
Risk
It can drift into "design portfolio." Fluorescent inks are hard to read as text, so body copy stays in Federal Blue.
Type
Big Shoulders Stencil (made for Chicago, a labor town) + Courier Prime.

DIRECTION 2

Dollhouse Diorama

The factory as a pop-up paper theater. Layers of construction paper cast real shadows. Characters are puppets on sticks, jointed with brass brads. The cutaway map becomes a dollhouse you look into.

Lineage
Wes Anderson's cutaway sets (the Life Aquatic ship, Grand Budapest), Paper Mario, Tearaway, the stagecraft of Kentucky Route Zero.
Why it spreads
Tactile and handmade, which is what short-video audiences reward. A parallax pan across the dollhouse map is a perfect 8-second clip. The symmetrical Wes Anderson set was its own meme format.
Theme fit
The proscenium frames everything as a performance: the happy factory is a show put on for visitors, and the trial is the one scene the workers stage themselves.
HTML cost
Medium. Layered SVG or photographed paper with CSS drop shadows. Parallax is almost free.
Risk
Cozy can soften the politics. The writing has to cut against the cuteness.
Type
Bagel Fat One + Patrick Hand.

DIRECTION 3

One-Bit, Candy-Color

The factory is black-and-white, dithered like an old Mac. Only the candy has color. Management owns the magic.

Each department you organize adds a second color, union teal, to the map. By Tour Day the drab factory is flooded with it. The art direction tracks your progress, and the moment it all turns teal is the clip people share.

Lineage
Return of the Obra Dinn, HyperCard and Decker stacks, World of Horror. Film's one-color devices: Schindler's List, Pleasantville.
Why it spreads
A visual idea you can explain in one sentence. 1-bit plus a single color is a longtime itch.io favorite and easy to spot in a thumbnail.
Theme fit
The strongest of the five. Color = power makes the thesis visible.
HTML cost
The lowest. Tiny, crisp art that's fast to draw, and the page stays light.
Risk
It can read as bleak or niche, and hides the candy-factory wonder at first. Small pixel fonts are hard to read on phones.
Type
Silkscreen + Pixelify Sans.

DIRECTION 4

Rubber-Hose Reel

1930s rubber-hose cartoons: sepia film grain, pie-cut eyes, white gloves, everything bouncing on the beat. Cheerful menace. Dialogue appears as silent-film title cards.

Lineage
Fleischer Studios, Cuphead, Bendy and the Ink Machine, the rubber-hose revival since Steamboat Willie entered the public domain in 2024.
Why it spreads
Cuphead proved the look alone can sell a game. Creepy-smiling corporate mascots are a proven hit with horror-comedy audiences.
Theme fit
Exactly the right era: the Flint sit-down strike was 1936–37, and Fleischer's own animators went on strike in 1937. The style comes from a labor fight.
HTML cost
The highest. This look depends on animation, and still frames look flat. Every character needs bounce cycles.
Risk
The animation budget, and constant Cuphead comparisons.
Type
Rye + Special Elite.

DIRECTION 5

Squishy Flat

Big flat pastel shapes, no outlines, bean-shaped characters with dot eyes and noodle legs. The jokes land through how the characters move.

Lineage
Pikuniku (a cheerful corporation exploits a village and you lead the rebellion, so it's a close cousin), Untitled Goose Game, Frog Detective, Wattam.
Why it spreads
Squash and stretch makes GIFs by itself. It's the most readable style on a phone, and streamers love this kind of cheerful chaos.
Theme fit
The weakest. It's funny and approachable, but the trial may lack teeth unless the color drains out when things get serious.
HTML cost
Low. It's all SVG and CSS, and animation is nearly free.
Risk
It looks like a lot of other cute indie games. It's the least distinctive of the five.
Type
Grandstander + Nunito.

Recommendation

Riso Union, using One-Bit's color rule

Combine the two strongest directions. Riso printing is literally built up one ink at a time, so the color-is-power idea from Direction 3 fits it naturally:

  • At the start, the factory is printed in a single ink: Federal Blue on newsprint. Drab and controlled.
  • Candy and management's magic get their own ink: fluorescent pink, which workers can't have.
  • Every department you organize adds a plate: union teal, then yellow. By Tour Day the whole print has come together.
  • Ending cards are full posters. The Cooperative ending uses every ink. The New Wonkmann ending prints in pink only.
If you agree, next steps
  1. Lock the name, the Day 1 outline and this direction.
  2. Scaffold the repo: index.html, inkjs, a map, scene and dialogue box styled in Riso.
  3. Build the vertical slice: Days 1–2, 4 rooms (Bunkhouse, River, Inventing, Candymen Office), 2 candies (wallpaper, Hair Toffee), Gramble and Toffo.
  4. Play it, tune the numbers, then commission or draw the real art.

Fallback if we want the smallest possible build: pure One-Bit, Candy-Color. It's the fastest art to make, and the color idea still carries it.
